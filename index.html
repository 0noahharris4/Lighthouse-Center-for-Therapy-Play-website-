About

For the past twelve years, I've worked alongside people carrying the weight of trauma — helping them process what happened, quiet a nervous system stuck on high alert, and reconnect with the people and goals that matter most to them. Good Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is trauma-focused therapy?

It's an approach that recognizes how difficult or overwhelming life experiences are and how they can continue to affect the mind and body long after the experience has ended. Rather than just discussing symptoms, we work together to process what happened at a pace that feels safe and helps to rebuild your sense of control.

What's the difference between CBT and client-centered therapy?

CBT is more structured, focusing on specific thought patterns and practical tools. Client-centered therapy is more open-ended, prioritizing warmth, acceptance, and your own pace. Most clients benefit from a blend of both, and we'll adjust that balance together as we go.

How do virtual sessions work?

We meet over Zoom, using a secure, HIPAA-compliant connection — all you need is a private space and a stable internet connection. Many clients find that being in their own environment actually makes it easier to open up.
